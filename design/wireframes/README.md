# Wireframes — ASCII bootstrap

These are ASCII sketches sufficient to pin down structure, hierarchy,
and copy. They are **not** the final design — Claude Design (or the
human UX designer) replaces them with proper wireframes / mockups in
this same directory.

Pinned by `design/HARLO_UX_BRIEF.md`. Do not deviate from the laws
in section 6 (interaction laws) of the brief without an Architect
role review.
